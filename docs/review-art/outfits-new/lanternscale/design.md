# Lanternscale Vest

Tate keeps his dark brown skin, black swept hair, athletic build and right-facing first idle sword stance. His hands are empty. Light comes from the upper left; highlights are solid pixels, with no emitted light.

The garment is the icon's sleeveless, high-collared vest: a dark oxblood leather foundation covered from chest to hip by staggered, overlapping, downward-pointing metal scales. Each scale has a dark red-brown interior, copper-orange lower face and narrow amber-gold rim, with sparse pale highlights. The overlapping tips make a short scalloped hem. The vest is compact and broad through the torso, with no long coat tails, cloak or hanging panels; the separated legs remain visible. This gives it a different outline from both Tate's black coat and the Ivory coat.

- **Collar:** a raised, open dark leather collar with a thin gold edge; the neck and a small dark underlayer remain visible inside it.
- **Shoulders:** rounded, gold-edged armholes and small overlapping scale edges, not large plate pauldrons. The fitted undersleeves remain visible.
- **Chest:** close-set rows of overlapping pointed copper scales, staggered so the dark seams and gold edges remain readable at sprite size.
- **Waist:** the same scale rows continue down to the hips and finish in distinct pointed, scalloped tips. No separate long belt or skirt.
- **Sleeves and gloves:** dark brown fitted cloth sleeves, simple gold wrist edging and dark leather gloves enclosing the empty fists.
- **Legs and boots:** dark brown fitted trousers and dark leather mid-calf boots with restrained brown highlights. No bright white panels.
