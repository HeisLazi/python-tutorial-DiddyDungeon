# Online section completion plan

## Product target

Build the online area for Lazi and two friends around a small shared, walkable **Shax Champions House**. It is an invite-only place to meet, shop, and launch the **Colosseum** or a cooperative raid. Friends should be able to join from the Windows and Linux desktop builds without installing Tailscale. This is a private learning game for three players, not public matchmaking or a commercial service.

The Colosseum name is confirmed. The hub name is **Shax Champions House**. The online merchant is **Sir Getrich** and the separate online currency is **Wazi Bucks**. Keep other local or joke-heavy names open for Lazi's review before shipping them.

## Current baseline

The PvP alpha already has private groups and invites, a walkable shared room with presence, Colosseum duel challenges, three duel modes, server-side code evaluation, ranked results and a private ladder. Linux and Windows test builds are available. A real-device playtest with two installed clients and friend accounts has not been completed yet.

Shax Champions House and Colosseum are now named in the app. PapaSmurff must be online for matches; full reboot recovery is deferred. See `PRIVATE_RELEASE_PLAN.md` for the alpha's verified build and host evidence.

## Scope and guardrails

- Support Lazi plus three invited friends, with no public matchmaking.
- Preserve each player's local campaign save. Online group activity must not silently rewrite it.
- Keep code execution on the isolated evaluator. A client may display results but cannot decide a correct answer, raid completion, reward, or ranked result.
- Treat host loss and reconnects as explicit states; never report a failed or incomplete task as passed.
- Keep reboot recovery out of this milestone unless Lazi reopens it.
- Keep macOS and a public launch out of scope.
- Keep the online currency separate from campaign coins. Raid bosses are its main source; PvP matches grant a smaller amount.
- Keep reward sources mode-specific so each activity has a reason to play. Campaign, sparring, and PvP can share eligible owned gear, while Infinite Dungeon has a separate run loadout and loot economy.
- Infinite Dungeon runs do not directly equip campaign gear. Players earn and choose weapons, armour, trinkets, and run upgrades from mobs, elites, and bosses during that run.
- Use the Infinite Dungeon Legacy Journal as the explicit bridge: permanent rewards earned there can unlock items usable outside the dungeon, and Journal progression can grant carefully bounded bonuses or choices inside future runs. Do not silently copy campaign inventory into a run.
- Mastery tiers are appearance unlocks over a player's existing eligible weapons/armour, not stat upgrades. Keep acquisition sources distinct even when an item can be equipped in multiple modes.
